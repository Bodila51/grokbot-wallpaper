Install in a minute.

Download, then three steps.

Windows

Windows 10 or 11

Download .zip For Lively Wallpaper (free)
  1. Install Lively Wallpaper. It is free on the Microsoft Store.
  2. Drag the zip into Lively. No need to unzip it.
  3. Set it as your wallpaper. Click Grok Bot in Lively’s library.

Mac

macOS 13 or later

Download .dmg Apple silicon and Intel If Chrome blocks it, download with Safari or choose Keep.
  1. Open the .dmg. It is in your Downloads.
  2. Drag Grok Bot Wallpaper to Applications.
  3. First launch: macOS says it can’t check the app. Click Done, then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click Open Anyway. The app isn’t notarized yet, so this happens once.On macOS 14 or earlier: right-click the app, then Open.

Show your bots. Have a bots channel? Paste it into the wallpaper’s settings and your bots appear beside Grok Bot as live cards. In Lively: Customise → Your bots channel. On a Mac: the 👀 menu-bar icon → Bots channel….
