Grok Bot Wallpaper

A live wallpaper that keeps an eye on your cursor and on your bots.

Install on Windows Try it full screen

What it does

Watches your cursor

Grok Bot’s eyes follow the mouse across the whole screen. Leave it alone and it looks around, blinks, and gets sleepy when your bots are resting.

Shows how your bots are doing

Live cards show which bots are working, which are waiting for you and which hit an error. When one needs you, Grok Bot glances at it.

Lets you move the cards

Drag the cards anywhere on the desktop and they stay where you leave them. Double-click a card to send it back.

Updates itself

The wallpaper loads from the web, so every new version reaches your desktop on its own.

Install on Windows

Free, about a minute, Windows 10 or 11.

  1. Get Lively Wallpaper

    A free app that runs web pages as your wallpaper. Install it from the Microsoft Store.

    Open Microsoft Store

  2. Add Grok Bot

    In Lively, click Add Wallpaper, paste the link below under Enter URL and press Enter.

https://bodila51.github.io/grokbot-wallpaper/face/

Eyes not following the cursor?

In Lively, open Settings, then Wallpaper, and set Wallpaper input to Mouse. Turn on Mouse interaction when application focused so it keeps watching over open windows.

A blue box when you drag a card?

That is Windows selecting desktop icons. Right-click the desktop, open View and untick Show desktop icons.
